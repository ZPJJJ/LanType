CHINESE AND ENGLISH INPUT DATA

1. Android-derived simplified Chinese pinyin words and frequencies:
   rime/rime-pinyin-simp, commit 0c6861ef7420ee780270ca6d993d18d4101049d0,
   https://github.com/rime/rime-pinyin-simp, Apache-2.0.
   The original AOSP-derived dictionary is retained, and used to generate
   local word, reading, glide and stroke-ranking indexes.
2. CC-CEDICT by the CC-CEDICT community, published by MDBG:
   https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary?page=cedict
   Snapshot 2026-11-16T07:16:40Z, CC-BY-SA-4.0.
   Changes: select simplified words, remove tone numbers from pinyin, remove
   duplicate word/reading pairs, assign a fixed fallback rank, and derive
   character reading/frequency indexes. These adaptations remain available
   under CC-BY-SA-4.0 with editable source and generation scripts.
3. SCOWL / English Speller Database by Kevin Atkinson and contributors:
   https://github.com/en-wl/wordlist-diff, rel-2026.02.25, en_US.txt.
   89,140 alphabetic US words are retained. The SCOWL Copyright file grants
   redistribution and sale subject to its notices. Original LanType editorial
   priorities replace corpus frequency estimates; trie and key indexes are
   generated by tools/lantype_resources/prepare_resources.py.
4. Cangjie5 character codes, Jackchows/Cangjie5 commit
   c9b64e77951cba48bdfa5de8eaedd1a908666b9b, MIT.
   Previous mixed-corpus weights/phrases were removed; character ranks now
   use the Android-derived dictionary. Quick codes use first/last code keys.
5. Five-stroke codes, rime/rime-stroke commit
   1e8fff9b9494ddec23b0cbc526bcfd8171a6fd48, LGPL-3.0.
   Codes are retained; ranking uses the declared Android/CC-CEDICT data,
   and short phrases use Android-derived words. Editable tables and scripts
   are supplied with the corresponding source.
6. McBopomofo Taiwan words, readings and tone tables, commit
   18e2060dd5d0b511753ad79a8fe0d112e944f1af, MIT; its BPMF mappings include
   libtabe material under BSD-3-Clause. Full McBopomofo Data README and
   libtabe copyright are included. Taiwan word ranks are reset to 1.
7. Auxiliary shape-code tables, HowcanoeWang/rime-lua-aux-code commit
   f9780033396f8998697c2a004d40bd543c46308b, MIT. Character=code data are
   converted to Rime tab-separated dictionaries; attribution is retained.
8. OpenCC conversion tables, BYVoid/OpenCC, Apache-2.0. Exact native source
   revision is pinned in the corresponding-source lock. CyimeTWPhrases uses
   the declared McBopomofo source. rime-ice schema/Lua code is GPL-3.0 from
   commit 9e66b0729083b37d217312294f6d516c8d7234be; its former word tables
   are replaced. Cangjie/Bopomofo schema adaptations retain LGPL notices.

OFFLINE MODELS AND HANDWRITING

9. OpenAI Whisper base multilingual model, MIT (code and weights),
   https://github.com/openai/whisper. Unmodified sherpa-onnx INT8 ONNX
   conversion by csukuangfj, revision bb53ee204431c90d314c1cc08d28d23e5b7927cc:
   https://huggingface.co/csukuangfj/sherpa-onnx-whisper-base.
   Encoder, decoder and tokens are bundled. The application exposes Chinese
   and English whole-sentence transcription; this is not streaming ASR.
10. Silero VAD by Silero Team, MIT, snakers4/silero-vad; sherpa-onnx export.
    Exact SHA-256 is in resources.lock.json. Used for local voice activity.
11. PP-OCRv5 mobile detection/recognition by PaddlePaddle contributors,
    ONNX distribution RapidAI/RapidOCR v3.9.2, Apache-2.0. Both models and
    the character dictionary are bundled; licenses/ocr contains full notices.
12. Chinese stroke medians from MakeMeAHanzi by skishore and contributors,
    commit bddc96d41bef78427ed0e034e9f7e31d71fd1b92. The graphics derive from
    Arphic fonts and remain under the Arphic Public License (1999).
    https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi. LanType normalizes/resamples
    the medians into a stroke-order template index for 9,574 characters;
    this modification and the original editable graphics data are supplied.
    Full Arphic license and MakeMeAHanzi copyright are retained. No Chinese
    dictionary from that project is used. Chinese templates are not original
    LanType handwriting and are not neural OCR weights.
13. The 62 Latin letter/digit stroke polylines, small association examples,
    ranking priorities, emoji/week-day/component mappings and synthesized
    key tones were authored for LanType and are dedicated under CC0-1.0.
    They do not contain MathWriting/OCHW/CASIA samples or recorded key audio.
    Recognition is single-character stroke-order matching, not cursive or
    formula recognition. Quality differs from the removed neural recognizer.

REMOVED ASSETS AND UPGRADE BEHAVIOR

This edition does not include SenseVoice, Paraformer, CAM++, OCHWPro/CASIA,
MathWriting samples, OpenSubtitles/LCCC corpus tables, Google-10000 lists,
ChaiPUA fonts, recorded keyboard sounds, external statistical sentence
scorers, or unreviewed game/topic dictionaries. Their old data are not
needed for these functions. Application-owned old Rime assets are retired
only when a precise known path, byte length and hash match; custom user
files/imported models are not deleted. Removed model selections fall back
to bundled Whisper, and new dictionaries trigger their own rebuilding.
